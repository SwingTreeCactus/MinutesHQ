Privacy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Minutes HQ helps small groups keep meeting minutes and follow up on what was decided. It is run by Mark Shahan as a free service for the groups that use it. This page explains what it stores, who can see it, and how to remove it.

What we store

  • Your Google sign-in: your name and email address, used only to sign you in. We never see your Google password, and the app asks Google for nothing else.
  • Your group: the name you give, whether you're an admin, and your reminder-email setting.
  • Your group's work: follow-up items (task, people assigned, due date), updates people post, the Decisions list from the latest meeting, and admins' private notes for the next meeting.

What stays on the device

Full meeting notes and agendas are kept encrypted on the admin's own tablet with the admin's passphrase. They are not stored on our servers. While a meeting runs, other admins of the same group can follow along; the notes pass to them directly and are not saved. If an admin chooses to use agenda reading or minutes tidying, that admin's tablet sends the agenda or notes to Anthropic for processing, under the admin's own account. Meetings marked Confidential are never sent.

Who can see what

Only people approved into your group see its names, items, updates and decisions. Admins also see members' email addresses, to manage reminders. Nobody outside your group can see your group's information. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone for advertising, and the app has no ads or tracking.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase stores the data above and handles sign-in.
  • Google confirms who you are when you sign in.
  • GitHub Pages serves the app's files.
  • Resend sends reminder emails, if your group uses them. Emails never contain item titles.
  • Anthropic processes agendas and notes only as described above.

On your device

The app stores your sign-in and its own files in your browser so it works offline. It uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

Removing your information

You can turn reminder emails off in Settings or with the link in any reminder. You can leave your group in Settings; your name stays on past items so the record makes sense, and an admin can delete it. An admin can delete the whole group, which removes all of its information. To have your sign-in and everything linked to it deleted, email the address below.
